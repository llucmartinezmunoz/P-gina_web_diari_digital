# Student Digital Newspaper 📰

## About the Project

A student-run digital newspaper created by a group of young people passionate about writing, critical thinking, and current affairs. The website provides a platform for students to express their ideas, share their perspectives, and engage in meaningful discussions through a variety of articles.

## Features

- **Personal Writing:** A space for students to share personal reflections and experiences.
- **Opinion Articles:** A section dedicated to opinions, arguments, and critical perspectives on different topics.
- **Digital Publishing:** An accessible online platform for publishing and reading articles.
- **Student-Led Initiative:** A collaborative project that encourages creativity, independent thinking, and student participation.

## Purpose

The goal of this project is to create an independent digital publication where students can share their work, explore different perspectives, and encourage intellectual discussion within the community.

## Technologies

This project was developed as a website for a digital newspaper. The website is deployed and hosted using **Netlify**.
